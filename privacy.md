# Privacy Policy — Groove

**Last updated:** August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Groove** ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your personal data when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data processed.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

We only collect and process data that is strictly necessary to provide and improve the functionality of the app:

* **Account & Authentication Data:** If you sign up or log in (e.g., using email/password, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In), we process your user ID and email address to manage your account.
* **Workout & Fitness Logs:** Any workout routines, sets, reps, weights, notes, and training progressions you input into the app are stored to provide your personal tracking history and analytics.
* **Device & Technical Information:** Basic technical data (such as device model, OS version, and app performance logs) may be processed to ensure stability and diagnose potential crashes.
* **Purchase Data:** Transaction identifiers and subscription statuses are processed to unlock premium features. **We do not collect or store your payment card details**; all financial transactions are handled securely by Apple via the App Store.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:
* To provide, maintain, and personalize your workout tracking experience.
* To manage your user account and verify your subscription status.
* To monitor app performance, fix bugs, and improve user experience.
* We **do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data** to any third parties or data brokers.

---

### 3. Third-Party Service Providers

To operate efficiently, Groove integrates trusted third-party services that may process data under their own privacy standards:

* **Firebase (Google LLC):** Used for backend database storage, user authentication, and app infrastructure.
* **RevenueCat:** Used to manage in-app purchases, subscriptions, and receipt validation.
* **Apple In-App Purchases:** Handles all payment processing and billing for digital subscriptions.

---

### 4. Data Storage & Retention

* Your data is stored securely on cloud servers provided by our infrastructure partners (Firebase) and/or locally on your device.
* We retain your personal information only as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the service.

---

### 5. Your Rights (GDPR & Privacy Rights)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR), you have the right to:
* Access the personal data we hold about you.
* Request the correction or deletion of your account and all associated data.
* Withdraw your consent at any time.

If you wish to delete your account or have your data removed, you can do so directly from within the app settings or by contacting us at the email address below.

---

### 6. Children’s Privacy

Groove is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
