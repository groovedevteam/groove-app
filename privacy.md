# Privacy Policy — Groove

**Last updated:** August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Groove** ("we", "our", "the app") handles your personal data when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data we process.

The data controller responsible for your personal data is **Matteo Alberto D'Andrade**, contactable as described in Section 11 below.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

We only collect and process data that is strictly necessary to provide and improve the functionality of the app:

* **Account & Authentication Data:** If you sign up or log in (e.g., using email/password, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In), we process your user ID and email address to manage your account.
* **Workout & Fitness Logs:** Any workout routines, sets, reps, weights, notes, and training progressions you input into the app are stored to provide your personal tracking history and analytics.
* **Apple Health Data (Special Category Data):** If you choose to connect Apple Health, and only after you grant explicit permission through Apple's own consent screen, Groove reads the following categories: weight, height, heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), sleep stages, active energy burned, water intake, and workout sessions. This data is used to power features such as your readiness score, workout duplicate detection, and progress tracking. Groove may also write workout sessions, weight, height, active energy, and water intake back to Apple Health. Raw Apple Health values are processed **locally on your device only** and are **not transmitted to or stored on our servers**. Only a boolean flag indicating whether Apple Health access is connected is stored on our servers, to remember your connection status across sessions. You can review, restrict, or revoke Groove's access to Apple Health at any time from *Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Groove* on your device.
* **Device & Technical Information:** Basic technical data (device model, OS version, and app performance/crash logs) is processed to ensure stability and diagnose bugs.
* **Purchase Data:** Transaction identifiers and subscription status are processed to unlock premium features. **We do not collect or store your payment card details**; all financial transactions are handled securely by Apple via the App Store and by our subscription management provider, RevenueCat.

---

### 2. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

* **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR):** for connecting Apple Health, which involves special category health data. This consent is requested separately from, and in addition to, your general acceptance of this policy, and is managed through Apple's system-level permission dialog. You may withdraw it at any time without affecting the rest of the app's functionality.
* **Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)):** to create your account, store your workout logs, and manage your subscription.
* **Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)):** for basic technical/crash diagnostics needed to keep the app stable and secure.

---

### 3. How We Use Your Information

* To provide, maintain, and personalize your workout tracking experience.
* To manage your user account and verify your subscription status.
* To generate AI-assisted workout plans when you use the "Generate with AI" feature.
* To monitor app performance, fix bugs, and improve user experience.
* The Readiness Score, Smart Coach suggestions, and AI-generated workout plans are automated outputs intended as informational guidance only. They do not constitute automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects on you within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR, and you are always free to disregard or modify them.
* **We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data** to any third parties or data brokers.

---

### 4. Third-Party Service Providers

Groove integrates the following trusted third-party services, each processing data under its own privacy standards:

* **Firebase (Google LLC):** used for backend database storage (Firestore), authentication, and Cloud Functions. We do **not** use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or any advertising/tracking SDK, and we do not use the Apple App Tracking Transparency framework because we do not track users across apps or websites.
* **RevenueCat:** management of in-app purchases, subscriptions, and receipt validation. RevenueCat receives transaction identifiers and subscription status, not payment card details.
* **Apple In-App Purchases:** handles all payment processing and billing for digital subscriptions.
* **Google AI Studio (Gemini):** generates personalized workout plans when you use the "Generate with AI" feature. Any training preferences, level information, or text/PDF/image you submit for this purpose is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate the plan.

We do not use Apple Health data as an input to any of the above third-party services; as noted in Section 1, Health data stays on-device except for the connection-status flag.

---

### 5. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers — including Firebase, RevenueCat, and Google AI Studio — may process and store data on servers located outside the European Union, including in the United States. Where this occurs, we rely on the safeguards provided by these providers, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent legal mechanisms recognized under GDPR, to ensure your data remains protected.

---

### 6. Data Storage & Retention

* Account, workout, and subscription data is stored on cloud servers provided by our infrastructure partner (Firebase) and/or locally on your device.
* We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active.
* If you delete your account, your account data and workout history are deleted from our servers within **30 days**, except where we are required to retain certain records (e.g., transaction records) for longer to comply with legal or tax obligations.
* Technical/crash logs are retained for a limited period (typically no longer than **90 days**) for diagnostic purposes and then automatically purged.
* Apple Health data is never stored on our servers, so no server-side retention period applies to it; retention on-device is governed by Apple's HealthKit and your own device settings.

---

### 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest (via our infrastructure provider, Firebase), restricted access controls, and secure authentication mechanisms. While no system can guarantee absolute security, we take reasonable steps to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

### 8. Your Rights (GDPR & Other Privacy Rights)

Depending on your jurisdiction, including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

* **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
* **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data.
* **Erase** your account and associated data ("right to be forgotten").
* **Restrict** processing in certain circumstances.
* **Port** your data to another provider in a structured, commonly used format.
* **Withdraw consent** at any time (e.g., disconnecting Apple Health), without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
* **Object** to processing based on legitimate interest.
* **Lodge a complaint** with your local data protection supervisory authority. If you reside in the EU, this is the authority of your country of residence (in Italy, the *Garante per la protezione dei dati personali*, www.garanteprivacy.it). If you reside outside the EU/UK, you may have equivalent rights under your local data protection law, and can contact us directly at the email below for any privacy request regardless of your location.

You can exercise most of these rights directly from within the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account, or Settings → Health), or by contacting us at the email address in Section 11. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law (generally within one month under GDPR).
